01

What Keirin is

Governed AI inside the cadence your teams already run

Keirin is an amplification layer, not a replacement. It doesn’t ask teams to abandon the agile framework they already run. It adds governed AI to every ceremony, with a human gate on each, so teams gain speed without giving up accountability.

AI drafts, analyzes and summarizes. People decide. Every AI use case earns its place on a one-page canvas before it enters a ceremony, and every gain is reported alongside its guardrails.

It plugs into
ScrumSAFeLeSSScrum@Scale
In keirin track cycling, a pacing bike, the derny, brings riders up to a speed they could not reach alone, then pulls off the track. The riders sprint for the line themselves. AI is the derny. It never finishes the race.

04

Rollout, middle-out

Top-down enablement, bottom-up proof

Phase 0WK 1–2

Diagnose

Baseline, opportunity heat map, draft Canvases, signed reinvestment agreement

Phase 1WK 3–8

Pilot

One volunteer team, about three sprints, before/after report

Phase 2WK 9–20

Scale

Waves of 2–3 teams, each funded by the last wave’s numbers

Phase 3WK 21–26

Institutionalize

Champion network owns the practice; governance moves to risk and quality

Executives make four decisions up front: sponsorship, budget and tooling, governance sign-off, and capacity reinvestment. Not a rollout mandate. Scaling waits for the pilot’s numbers.
